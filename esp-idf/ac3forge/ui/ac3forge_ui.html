    Speakers

    Wiring

    Two lines carry twice the slots of one. It takes effect at the next play.

    Slot width

    The speakers this player drives, one per output slot: a name such as 5.1 or 7.1.4, or one speaker per slot such as L,R,C,LFE,Ls,Rs. It takes effect at the next play.

    What an output layout does

    2.0 or 1.0: the decoder folds every channel but the LFE into two channels, or one, heights and rear surrounds included.

    Wider: each channel plays on the speaker at its location. A channel the layout has no speaker for is spread over the nearest ones, and the LFE goes only to LFE outputs.

    With height speakers, a stream's objects are placed by their positions.

    Nothing is upmixed. A speaker the stream has nothing for stays silent, so a 5.1 stream on 7.1.4 leaves the rear surrounds and heights silent; Now lists them.
