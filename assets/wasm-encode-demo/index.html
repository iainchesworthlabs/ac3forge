ac3forge - WASM encode demo

A real AC-3/E-AC-3 encoder, compiled to WebAssembly and running entirely in this tab: no server-side encode, no upload. Drop a .wav file - mono, stereo, 5.1, or the wide E-AC-3 layouts 7.1 / 5.1.4 / 7.1.4 - or record live from the microphone, and get back a real elementary stream carrying a measured dialnorm, plus a loudness/true-peak QC verdict against five delivery presets - all computed on your machine. There is also an Atmos object-authoring demo: pan real audio objects around a room and encode the result as E-AC-3 + JOC, live.
