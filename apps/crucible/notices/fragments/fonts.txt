Typefaces
---------

ExtraBold), Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors
(https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo), and Noto Sans Arabic and Noto
Sans Hebrew, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors
(https://github.com/notofonts/arabic and https://github.com/notofonts/hebrew),
for the languages Archivo does not cover. Each is licensed under the SIL
Open Font License, Version 1.1, reproduced below as it ships with Archivo;
the same text, under the Noto copyright lines above, governs the two Noto
faces.
