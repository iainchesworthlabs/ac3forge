Qt {{QT_VERSION}}
------------------

This package includes the Qt {{QT_VERSION}} libraries, plugins, QML modules
and translations that ac3crucible.exe loads (bin/Qt6*.dll, plugins/, qml/
and translations/): Qt Core, Gui, Widgets, Network, OpenGL, Qml (with its
Models, WorkerScript and Meta libraries), Quick, Quick Controls 2 and its
styles, Quick Dialogs 2, Quick Effects, Quick Layouts, Quick Shapes, Quick
Templates 2, Quick Test and Test, Svg, Shader Tools, Qt Labs Platform and
FolderListModel, and the platform, image-format, icon-engine, TLS,
network-information, style and QML-tooling plugins.

These are used under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3,
reproduced at the end of this section; the GNU General Public License it
refers to is LICENSE.txt beside this file. Qt's translations are under the
BSD 3-clause licence. The module for the room's 3D view, where this build
carries it, is under different terms and has its own section below.

The Qt libraries are linked dynamically. You may replace them with a
modified Qt 6 that is binary-compatible with this version and the
application will load it: they are looked up by name from the directories
bin/qt.conf points at.

by The Qt Company at

issue tracker, for as long as this package is offered.
