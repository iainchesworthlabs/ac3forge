ac3forge - WASM Atmos authoring demo

Author a real Dolby Atmos (E-AC-3 + JOC) stream in this tab: drag audio objects around a top-down room while the page encodes, and every drag becomes OAMD position metadata in the stream - one placement per frame, so the motion you draw is the pan a decoder renders. Objects are synthesised tones (this is an authoring demo for the metadata path, not a mixing console); the encoder underneath is ac3::oba::AtmosEncoder, the same class ac3cli atmos-encode uses.

Loading WASM modules...

Room (top-down - front of the room at the top)

Positions are TS 103 420 §4.2.1 room-anchored coordinates: x left→right, y front→back, z floor→ceiling (the height slider). Drag a dot to pan its object; the placement each 1536-sample frame is encoded with is whatever the room shows at that instant.

Result

The preview decodes the stream this page just authored through the same WASM decode module the decode demo uses - the objects you panned, reconstructed from the E-AC-3 + JOC bytes. The downloaded .ec3 also plays in the decode demo and in ac3cli decode.
