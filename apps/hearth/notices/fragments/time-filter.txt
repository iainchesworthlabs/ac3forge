Sendspin time filter
--------------------

Sendspin's reference time filter is compiled into Hearth's programs, whose
players map a server's clock to their own with it. It is used unmodified,
under the Apache License, version 2.0, from github.com/Sendspin/time-filter
at the commit that the source's VENDORED.md names.
