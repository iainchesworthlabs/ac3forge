Material Symbols
-----------------

Hearth's programs embed eight glyphs (play, pause, stop, skip previous,
skip next, create new folder, add and volume up) from Google's Material
Symbols Sharp icon font (github.com/google/material-design-icons), Copyright
Google Inc., subset to just those glyphs for the transport bar and queue
controls. It is used under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
